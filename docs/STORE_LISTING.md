## Full description
**One post. One folder. Every image together.**

PostPocket turns messy Facebook image collections into a clean, searchable library that lives entirely on your computer.

- Save every image from a Facebook post into its own folder, in order
- Preview and select images before downloading
- Scan the current post, several loaded posts, or use a post URL queue
- Import local images, folders, ZIPs, or your own Facebook data archive
- Name folders and files your way with templates and tokens
- Manage collections with search, filters, favorites, tags and a recycle bin
- Detect duplicate images with local SHA-256 hashes
- Export one ZIP, a ZIP per post, real nested folders, CSV or JSON
- Pause, resume, retry and recover downloads — even after Chrome restarts
- Choose between a toolbar popup, a side panel and a full dashboard

**Privacy first.** Everything is processed on your device. There is no account, no backend, no analytics and no cloud sync. PostPocket never asks for your Facebook password, cookies or tokens, and does not use the cookies API.

**Important:** PostPocket is a user-directed organizer. It only reads the post you are viewing or files you choose yourself. It does not crawl Facebook and cannot bypass access controls.
